# CUMCM official format baseline

This file records the 2026 national baseline verified on 2026-09-04. Recheck the current national, regional, and school notices before final submission. A newer applicable source supersedes this file.

## National requirements

### Paper copy

- Use white A4 paper, single- or double-sided; leave at least 2.5 cm on every side; bind on the left.
- Page 1 is the commitment form, page 2 the numbering page, and page 3 the abstract page.
- The abstract page contains title, abstract, and keywords; no English translation is required; it should in principle fit on one page.
- Number pages from the abstract page as page 1, using centered Arabic numerals in the footer.
- Body text begins on paper page 4, contains no table of contents, and is at most 30 pages. Appendices follow and have no page limit.
- The abstract page, body, and appendix must not reveal contestant, school, or region identity.
- Cite every external or public result at its use location and include it in the references.

### Appendix and support material

- The appendix lists all support-material files and includes all complete, runnable source programs, including interactive commands used in Excel or SPSS.
- If no program was used, state `本论文没有用到程序` in the appendix.
- Submit the paper and support material as two electronic files.
- The electronic paper is one uncompressed PDF or Word file, preferably PDF, at most 20 MB. It excludes the commitment and numbering pages, so its first page is the abstract page.
- The electronic paper must match the paper copy, including appendices.
- The support archive is one RAR or ZIP at most 20 MB. It contains necessary runnable programs, externally collected data, and substantial intermediate results/figures. Its file list also appears in the appendix.
- If there is no support material, the archive may be omitted and the appendix must state `本论文没有支撑材料`.
- Support files must not reveal contestant, school, or region identity and must agree with the paper.

### AI use in 2026

- Put an `AI 工具使用声明` before the references and use the official statement matching actual use.
- If AI was used, briefly state its purpose in the paper and include `AI工具使用详情.pdf` in support material.
- That PDF records tool/model, purpose and stage, principal prompting/use process, and adoption, manual modification, and verification of outputs, except that pure language polishing need not describe adoption details.
- The team must lead core modeling and analysis and individually review and verify AI-generated content.

## Not nationally standardized

The 2026 national specification does not standardize font, font size, line spacing, or color. Do not label Song typeface, small-four size, 1.5 line spacing, a particular title size, three-line table width, citation-style edition, or palette as a national requirement. They may be sensible defaults only after checking local notices.

## Audit output

For each finding, record the exact rule source/version, observed evidence, status (`pass`, `fail`, or `manual`), and remediation. Do not infer page margins, formula correctness, or visual readability from text extraction alone.
